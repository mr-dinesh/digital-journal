Data: Oikolab hourly reanalysis for Bangalore, processed and published daily by Karthik S at weather.karthiks.co (source, MIT). This page re-reads that file every few hours.

Normal is the median high or low for that calendar date over 1981 to now, the same definition as the upstream chart. Record is the highest high or lowest low for that date in any other year. A hot day beats the 90th percentile of highs for dates within a week either side. A rainy day has at least 2.5 mm (the IMD definition). Normal rain is the mean for that date, smoothed over ±7 days.
