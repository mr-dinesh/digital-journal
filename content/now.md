## Building

The Vibecoding series carries on. It started as one project a day. That lasted about as long as most of my New Year's resolutions. Now it's one when it's ready, which was the right call. The point was never the count.

{{< now-auto "building" >}}

September and October were quiet. My home lab computer kept switching itself off whenever it worked hard. It took a new processor and some gentle persuasion to calm it down. Then four projects came out in the first week of November, as if to make up for lost time.

Most of my AI projects now use Cloudflare's own AI service, so visitors no longer need an AI key of their own. A weekly check warns me before any AI model one of them depends on is retired, instead of me finding out when something breaks.

Older ones still running: a mandala drawing tool at [/mandala](/mandala/), an internet radio at [/radio](/radio/), my own short links at [s.mrdee.in](https://s.mrdee.in/), a reader that strips trackers out of articles, and a little mindfulness check-in that keeps nothing it doesn't need.

Type a wrong address on this site and you'll get a random Rajnikanth joke instead of a boring error. Priorities.

## Learning

How Linux works at its core. In July I got my lab computer properly up to date and set it up so I can build and test my own version of Linux safely, in about a second, without wrecking anything. My [90-day plan toward a first contribution](/notes/kernel-lab-and-90-day-plan/) is on hold, because the computer trouble ate September and October.

Fuzzing, which means finding bugs by throwing millions of slightly broken inputs at a program. I've been trying it on curl, after spending a while reading how curl works. The same work on Bitwarden is parked until the lab computer can be trusted to run for days without a tantrum.

Teaching, a little. AB has started a BA in English. A [Telegram study buddy](/vibecoding/vibecoding-028-study-buddy-bot/) sends him a short spoken lesson each morning and checks his answer in the evening. It's more patient than I am.
